How to play

One string

  • Show both hands and pinch thumb and index finger on each. A string appears between them.
  • Move your hands apart for a lower note, together for a higher one.
  • Raise the string for louder, lower it for quieter. Tilt it to make the tone brighter or mellower.
  • Wiggle a hand for vibrato (or a re-pluck on Guitar and Bells). Let go to twang it away.

Floating string

  • Turn on Float, pinch both hands, pull them apart, then let go. The string stays in the air.
  • Pluck it with any finger of either hand. Where you pluck decides the note: low on the left, high on the right.
  • Make a new one to replace it, or turn Float off to clear it.

Five strings

  • Press your hands together like a prayer, then pull them apart.
  • Thumb to pinky play C, D, E, G, A. Hands closer together plays an octave higher.
  • Wiggle a finger to pluck its string. Bring your hands back together to reset.
  • Curl the same finger on both hands to remove that string and its note. Open both again to bring it back.
  • Duet: while someone holds the five strings, a friend can pluck them by sweeping their fingers across the strings. Up to four hands are tracked.

Backing music

  • Open Music and pick Orchestra (strings, cellos, violins, flute, brass, timpani) or EDM (kick, hats, bass, synth stabs and a lead). Pick Off to stop.
  • Raise your hands for louder, lower them for softer. Spread your hands wide for more energy: brass and drums, or claps, stabs and lead.
  • Move your hands faster to speed it up, hold still to let it slow down.
  • You can still pinch, pull out the five strings, or pluck as a duet over the top.

Buttons

  • Instrument: Synth, Bells or Guitar.
  • Music: pick the backing music. Sound: mute.
  • Visuals: pick a look: Neon (electric), Candy (sweet and sparkly), Ocean (watery), Fire (flames and embers), Space (comets and stars) or Chakra (seven glowing energy centres). Anim Off keeps just a plain glowing string and turns off the extra effects, which helps if things feel laggy. Full: full screen.

Tips

  • Use good light and keep both hands in view of the front camera.
  • Everything stays on your device. Nothing is recorded.
