How to play

One string

  • Show both hands and pinch thumb and index finger on each. A string appears between them.
  • Move your hands apart for a lower note, together for a higher one.
  • Raise the string for louder, lower it for quieter. Tilt it to make the tone brighter or mellower.
  • Wiggle a hand for vibrato (or a re-pluck on Harp, Guitar and Bells). Let go to twang it away.

Five strings

  • Press your hands together like a prayer, then pull them apart.
  • Thumb to pinky play C, D, E, G, A. Hands closer together plays an octave higher.
  • Wiggle a finger to pluck its string. Bring your hands back together to reset.

Buttons

  • Instrument: Synth, Violin, Harp, Guitar or Bells.
  • Sound: mute. Snap: lock notes to a pleasant scale, or glide freely.
  • Hands: show or hide the skeleton. Colour: change theme. Full: full screen.

Tips

  • Use good light and keep both hands in view of the front camera.
  • Everything stays on your device. Nothing is recorded.
